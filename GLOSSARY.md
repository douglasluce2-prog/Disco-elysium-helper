## Skills

### Ancient Reptilian Brain

*Also: Reptilian Brain*

**A primal inner voice (not one of the 24 skills): deep, slow and comforting about oblivion.**

The oldest part of your brain. It speaks in the darkest moments, such as the very first lines of the game and when you are close to death, and it tempts you to give up and sink back into nothing.

See also: [Limbic System](#limbic-system), [Spinal Cord](#spinal-cord)

### Authority

**Psyche skill: asserting yourself and commanding respect.**

Authority is the voice that demands obedience and gets offended when you're disrespected. It helps you intimidate and take control of a conversation, and it tends to push you toward shouting.

See also: [Psyche](#psyche), [Half Light](#half-light), [Physical Instrument](#physical-instrument)

### Composure

**Motorics skill: poker face, body language and keeping it together in public.**

Composure controls how you hold yourself: not flinching, not crying, reading other people's body language. It also has strong opinions about clothing. Very high Composure can make it hard to show any emotion at all.

See also: [Motorics](#motorics), [Drama](#drama), [Savoir Faire](#savoir-faire)

### Conceptualization

**Intellect skill: creativity and art. Sees artistic meaning and comes up with original ideas.**

Conceptualization judges art, invents metaphors and proposes creative solutions. It is often grandiose and harshly critical of anything it considers unoriginal.

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Inland Empire](#inland-empire)

### Drama

**Intellect skill: acting and lying, and telling when others are lying.**

Drama is your inner actor. It speaks in a theatrical, flowery way. It helps you bluff and warns you when someone is putting on a performance, but it can also make you paranoid and melodramatic.

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Empathy](#empathy), [Composure](#composure)

### Electrochemistry

**Physique skill: appetites. Drugs, alcohol, sex, and knowing how to party.**

Electrochemistry speaks for your brain chemistry and cravings. It knows everything about substances and pleasure, encourages you to indulge, and is very persuasive about it. It is a big part of why the detective is in the state he is in.

See also: [Physique](#physique), [Volition](#volition)

### Empathy

**Psyche skill: understanding how other people feel.**

Empathy reads emotions behind words and faces: who is grieving, who is ashamed, who is hiding pain. It makes you kinder, and also more easily hurt.

See also: [Psyche](#psyche), [Drama](#drama), [Suggestion](#suggestion)

### Encyclopedia

**Intellect skill: your store of trivia about history, science and culture.**

Encyclopedia is the voice that explains the world: who a historical figure was, what a political movement believed, what a building used to be. It is one of the best sources of lore in the game, but it often recalls useless facts while forgetting important personal ones (like your own name).

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Logic](#logic), [Rhetoric](#rhetoric)

### Endurance

**Physique skill: stamina and toughness. Sets how much Health you have.**

Endurance keeps you going on no sleep and a ruined body. Your maximum Health depends on it.

See also: [Physique](#physique), [Health](#health), [Pain Threshold](#pain-threshold)

### Esprit de Corps

**Psyche skill: cop culture and loyalty to your fellow officers. Shows you glimpses of them elsewhere.**

Esprit de Corps understands what it means to be police. Its special trick is the 'flash-sideways': short scenes showing what other officers are doing or thinking at that moment, far away from you.

*Real-world inspiration:* French for 'spirit of the body (corps)': the morale and loyalty that bind members of a group, especially soldiers or police.

See also: [Psyche](#psyche), [Revachol Citizens Militia](#revachol-citizens-militia), [Kim Kitsuragi](#kim-kitsuragi)

### Half Light

**Physique skill: fight-or-flight. Fear, aggression and sensing danger.**

Half Light is the animal alarm system. It warns you of threats, makes you jumpy and suspicious, and urges you to strike first. It is useful in danger and dangerous in conversation.

See also: [Physique](#physique), [Authority](#authority), [Reaction Speed](#reaction-speed)

### Hand/Eye Coordination

*Also: Hand-Eye Coordination, Hand Eye Coordination*

**Motorics skill: aim and precision. Shooting, throwing and steady hands.**

Hand/Eye Coordination is about accuracy: firing a gun, throwing something, doing delicate work. It is fond of guns.

See also: [Motorics](#motorics), [Reaction Speed](#reaction-speed)

### Inland Empire

**Psyche skill: imagination, hunches and the uncanny. Lets you 'hear' objects and dreams.**

Inland Empire is the mystical, dreamlike part of your mind. It gives inanimate things voices (your necktie, even a corpse), offers gut feelings that turn out to be true, and drifts into the surreal. It is often right in ways that make no logical sense.

*Real-world inspiration:* Almost certainly named after David Lynch's 2006 film 'Inland Empire', known for its dream logic. (The Inland Empire is also a real region of California.)

See also: [Psyche](#psyche), [Horrific Necktie](#horrific-necktie), [Shivers](#shivers), [Conceptualization](#conceptualization)

### Intellect

**Attribute: raw brain power. Governs Logic, Encyclopedia, Rhetoric, Drama, Conceptualization and Visual Calculus.**

One of your four attributes. Your Intellect score sets the starting level and the maximum level of its six skills. High-Intellect characters notice contradictions, remember trivia and win arguments, but can lose touch with their feelings and their body.

See also: [Psyche](#psyche), [Physique](#physique), [Motorics](#motorics), [Skill check](#skill-check)

### Interfacing

**Motorics skill: machines and mechanisms. Locks, radios, radiocomputers.**

Interfacing understands how things work with your hands: picking locks, fixing devices, operating radiocomputers. It also gives you an almost tactile sense of machinery.

See also: [Motorics](#motorics), [Radiocomputer](#radiocomputer)

### Limbic System

**A primal inner voice (not one of the 24 skills): the raw, hurting emotional core of your mind.**

The Limbic System speaks for old pain and fear. It appears at the start of the game alongside the Ancient Reptilian Brain, and in moments of emotional crisis. It is cruel because it knows exactly what hurts.

See also: [Ancient Reptilian Brain](#ancient-reptilian-brain), [Spinal Cord](#spinal-cord)

### Logic

**Intellect skill: reasoning and deduction. Spots contradictions and connects facts.**

Logic is the cold, analytical voice. It points out when a story doesn't add up and pieces evidence together. Turned up too high it can make you dismiss anything that isn't strictly rational.

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Visual Calculus](#visual-calculus), [Encyclopedia](#encyclopedia)

### Motorics

**Attribute: coordination and senses. Governs Hand/Eye Coordination, Perception, Reaction Speed, Savoir Faire, Interfacing and Composure.**

One of your four attributes. Reflexes, fine motor control, noticing details, style and keeping a straight face.

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Psyche](#psyche), [Physique](#physique)

### Pain Threshold

**Physique skill: withstanding pain, physical and emotional.**

Pain Threshold helps you shrug off injuries and keep going. It has a darker side: it can come to enjoy pain and push you toward things that hurt.

See also: [Physique](#physique), [Endurance](#endurance), [Health](#health)

### Perception

**Motorics skill: noticing things with your senses: sight, hearing, smell and taste.**

Perception catches the small details others miss: a smell in the air, something half-hidden, a sound in the distance. It is one of the most frequently heard voices.

See also: [Motorics](#motorics), [Visual Calculus](#visual-calculus)

### Physical Instrument

**Physique skill: raw strength and physical presence.**

Physical Instrument is your inner gym coach: loud, blunt and convinced every problem can be solved with muscles. It helps with lifting, breaking and intimidating.

See also: [Physique](#physique), [Authority](#authority), [Half Light](#half-light)

### Physique

**Attribute: the body. Governs Endurance, Pain Threshold, Physical Instrument, Electrochemistry, Shivers and Half Light.**

One of your four attributes. Strength, toughness and appetites. Your Health (your physical health bar) comes from this side of you.

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Psyche](#psyche), [Motorics](#motorics), [Health](#health)

### Psyche

**Attribute: emotional intelligence and willpower. Governs Volition, Inland Empire, Empathy, Authority, Esprit de Corps and Suggestion.**

One of your four attributes. It covers reading people, influencing them and keeping yourself together. Your Morale (your mental health bar) comes from this side of you.

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Physique](#physique), [Motorics](#motorics), [Morale](#morale)

### Reaction Speed

**Motorics skill: reflexes and quick wits.**

Reaction Speed lets you dodge, catch and answer fast. It also notices quick changes, like someone's expression flickering.

See also: [Motorics](#motorics), [Half Light](#half-light)

### Rhetoric

**Intellect skill: argument, debate and politics. Picks apart what people say.**

Rhetoric helps you win debates, notice loaded words and recognise political positions. It loves ideology, and it can make you rigid and argumentative.

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Drama](#drama), [Communism](#communism), [Ultraliberalism](#ultraliberalism), [Moralism](#moralism), [Fascism](#fascism)

### Savoir Faire

**Motorics skill: style and agility. Jumping, sneaking, stealing, and looking cool doing it.**

Savoir Faire wants you to be effortlessly cool. It handles acrobatics, sleight of hand and flair, and it is crushed when you embarrass yourself.

*Real-world inspiration:* French for 'knowing how to do': social grace, poise and confidence.

See also: [Motorics](#motorics), [Composure](#composure)

### Shivers

**Physique skill: a supernatural connection to the city. Lets you sense what is happening elsewhere.**

Shivers lets the city of Revachol speak to you. When it triggers, you feel the wind and the cold and suddenly perceive scenes somewhere else in the district. Its voice is often written as if the city itself is talking.

See also: [Physique](#physique), [Revachol](#revachol), [Inland Empire](#inland-empire)

### Spinal Cord

**A primal inner voice (not one of the 24 skills): pure, thoughtless physical action.**

The Spinal Cord speaks rarely, when the body takes over completely and thinking stops.

See also: [Ancient Reptilian Brain](#ancient-reptilian-brain), [Limbic System](#limbic-system)

### Suggestion

**Psyche skill: charm and soft manipulation. Getting people to do what you want.**

Suggestion is the smooth talker. Where Authority pushes, Suggestion persuades: it knows what to say to make someone like you or agree with you.

See also: [Psyche](#psyche), [Empathy](#empathy), [Authority](#authority)

### Visual Calculus

**Intellect skill: spatial reasoning. Reconstructs crime scenes, angles and trajectories.**

Visual Calculus turns a scene into geometry: where a shot came from, how a body fell, what distance something was thrown. It is the forensic, almost mathematical part of your detective work.

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Logic](#logic), [Perception](#perception)

### Volition

**Psyche skill: willpower and self-respect. Keeps your Morale up and resists temptation.**

Volition is the steady voice that tells you to pull yourself together, stop drinking and treat yourself (and others) decently. It also protects your Morale. If the other voices are a crowd, Volition is the one trying to keep order.

See also: [Psyche](#psyche), [Morale](#morale), [Electrochemistry](#electrochemistry)

## Game Mechanics

### Archetype

*Also: archetypes*

**A ready-made starting character (Thinker, Sensitive or Physical) offered at the start.**

The archetypes are preset spreads of attribute points: the Thinker leans on Intellect, the Sensitive on Psyche, the Physical on Physique. You can also build your own detective.

See also: [Intellect](#intellect), [Psyche](#psyche), [Physique](#physique), [Signature skill](#signature-skill)

### Check difficulty

*Also: Trivial, Challenging, Formidable, Legendary, Heroic, Godly*

**The named difficulty levels on skill checks, from Trivial up to Impossible.**

From easiest to hardest: Trivial (6), Easy (8), Medium (10), Challenging (12), Formidable (13), Legendary (14), Heroic (15), Godly (16), Impossible (18 and up). The number is the total you need on two dice plus your skill and bonuses.

See also: [Skill check](#skill-check)

### Copotype

*Also: copotypes*

**The kind of cop your choices make you, e.g. Superstar Cop, Sorry Cop, Apocalypse Cop, Boring Cop.**

Like your politics, your style of policing is tracked: grandiose and showy (Superstar Cop), forever apologising (Sorry Cop), obsessed with the end of the world (Apocalypse Cop), sensible and by-the-book (Boring Cop), and others. Each comes with its own reactions and Thoughts.

See also: [Political alignment](#political-alignment)

### Health

**Your physical health bar. If it hits zero, you die and the game ends.**

Health is lost through injuries and physical strain and comes from Endurance. You can restore it with healing items. Keep an eye on both Health and Morale: losing either one ends the game.

See also: [Morale](#morale), [Endurance](#endurance), [Physique](#physique)

### Internalize

*Also: internalizing, internalized, internalise, internalising, internalised*

**To research a Thought in your Thought Cabinet until it becomes part of you.**

See also: [Thought Cabinet](#thought-cabinet), [Thought](#thought)

### Morale

**Your mental health bar. If it hits zero, you give up on being a cop and the game ends.**

Morale is lost through humiliation, despair and emotional blows. It comes from Volition. Like Health, it can be restored with certain items.

See also: [Health](#health), [Volition](#volition), [Psyche](#psyche)

### Orb

*Also: orbs*

**The small white bubbles that appear over things in the world. Click one to hear a skill's comment.**

Orbs are passive observations tied to a place or object. Walking near something can trigger one; clicking it plays a short line from one of your skills. Hold Tab to highlight everything you can interact with nearby.

See also: [Passive check](#passive-check)

### Passive check

*Also: passive checks*

**An automatic check you never see rolled. It's why skills suddenly interrupt with comments.**

Passive checks compare your skill level to a hidden difficulty without rolling dice. If your skill is high enough, that skill chimes in during conversation with an observation or idea. Higher skills therefore mean that voice speaks more often.

See also: [Skill check](#skill-check)

### Political alignment

*Also: political alignments, political vision*

**The game tracks which political direction your answers lean: communist, ultraliberal, moralist or fascist.**

Many dialogue options are tagged with a political leaning. Picking them shifts your detective's politics, unlocks related Thoughts, and in The Final Cut can open political 'vision quests'. None of them is required, and the game satirises all four.

See also: [Communism](#communism), [Ultraliberalism](#ultraliberalism), [Moralism](#moralism), [Fascism](#fascism), [Copotype](#copotype)

### Red check

*Also: red checks*

**A one-time skill check. If you fail it, you can never retry it.**

Red checks are shown in red. They are moments that happen once, so the result sticks. Failing is not a game over: failures often lead to interesting (or funny) outcomes, and the story continues.

See also: [Skill check](#skill-check), [White check](#white-check)

### Signature skill

**The one skill you picked at character creation. It gets a bonus and a higher maximum.**

See also: [Skill point](#skill-point), [Archetype](#archetype)

### Skill check

*Also: skill checks*

**A dice roll: two six-sided dice plus your skill level, against a difficulty number.**

When you pick a dialogue option marked like [Logic - Medium 10], the game rolls two dice (2d6), adds your Logic and any bonuses, and you succeed if the total reaches the target. Rolling double sixes always succeeds, and double ones always fails. Hover over a check before choosing it to see your exact chance.

See also: [White check](#white-check), [Red check](#red-check), [Passive check](#passive-check), [Check difficulty](#check-difficulty)

### Skill point

*Also: skill points*

**Earned each time you level up. Spend it to raise a skill or unlock a Thought Cabinet slot.**

You gain experience (XP) for progressing the story and passing checks, and each level up gives you a skill point. Each skill has a maximum (its cap) that depends on its attribute and on whether it is your signature skill. Raising a skill also unlocks failed white checks for it.

See also: [White check](#white-check), [Thought Cabinet](#thought-cabinet), [Signature skill](#signature-skill)

### Thought

*Also: thoughts*

**An idea you can place in the Thought Cabinet to research.**

Thoughts are unlocked by things you say and do. Each one has a research time, an effect while you are thinking about it, and a different effect once it is complete.

See also: [Thought Cabinet](#thought-cabinet), [Internalize](#internalize)

### Thought Cabinet

**A menu of ideas you can 'internalize': think about over time to gain permanent effects.**

During the game you discover Thoughts: ideas, obsessions, ideologies. Place one in a free slot of the Thought Cabinet and it is researched over in-game time (usually with a temporary effect). When it finishes, you get its final, permanent effect. Some are useful, some are jokes, some change how characters treat you. You can unlock more slots with skill points, and forget a thought later if you need the slot.

See also: [Thought](#thought), [Internalize](#internalize), [Skill point](#skill-point)

### White check

*Also: white checks*

**A skill check you can retry later if you fail, after improving the skill involved.**

White checks are shown in white. If one fails, it locks until you put a point into that skill (or something relevant changes), then you can try again. They are safe to attempt.

See also: [Skill check](#skill-check), [Red check](#red-check), [Skill point](#skill-point)

## People

### Cuno

**A foul-mouthed kid who throws rocks at the hanging body and talks about himself in the third person.**

Cuno is a kid: aggressive and constantly swearing ('Cuno doesn't care'). He hangs around near the Whirling with his friend Cunoesse. Underneath the bluster he is a neglected child.

See also: [Cunoesse](#cunoesse), [Pig](#pig)

### Cunoesse

**Cuno's friend and partner in crime: just as foul-mouthed.**

See also: [Cuno](#cuno)

### Evrart Claire

*Also: Evrart*

**The head of the Martinaise dockworkers' union. Enormous, friendly and very powerful.**

Evrart effectively runs Martinaise from the harbour. He is warm, flattering and always talking about doing favours, which you will be expected to return. He is leading the strike against the Wild Pines company.

See also: [Union](#union), [Harbour](#harbour), [Wild Pines](#wild-pines), [Strike](#strike)

### Garte

*Also: Cafeteria Manager, Garte the Cafeteria Manager*

**The manager of the Whirling-in-Rags. You owe him money for your room (and the damage).**

Garte runs the hostel-cafeteria where you wake up. He is tired, sarcastic and mostly wants to be paid.

See also: [Whirling-in-Rags](#whirling-in-rags), [réal](#réal)

### Gaston Martin

*Also: Gaston*

**René Arnoux's elderly friend and pétanque partner.**

See also: [Pétanque](#pétanque)

### Horrific Necktie

*Also: necktie*

**A hideous tie you can wear. It talks to you (through Inland Empire) and encourages bad decisions.**

The necktie is found in your room at the very start. If you put it on, it becomes a voice of its own: a party animal that cheers on your worst impulses.

See also: [Inland Empire](#inland-empire), [Electrochemistry](#electrochemistry)

### Jean Vicquemare

*Also: Jean*

**An officer from Harry's own Precinct 41 who has very little patience left for him.**

Jean has worked with Harry for years and has seen the worst of him. He is bitter, blunt and tired of covering for his colleague.

See also: [Precinct 41](#precinct-41), [Judit Minot](#judit-minot)

### Joyce Messier

*Also: Joyce*

**The negotiator sent by the Wild Pines company to deal with the dockworkers' strike.**

Joyce is wealthy, sharp and surprisingly candid. She is one of the best people in the game to ask about Revachol's politics and history, how the city is governed and who holds power in Martinaise.

See also: [Wild Pines](#wild-pines), [Strike](#strike), [Mercenaries](#mercenaries)

### Judit Minot

*Also: Judit*

**An officer of Precinct 41 who works alongside Jean Vicquemare.**

See also: [Precinct 41](#precinct-41), [Jean Vicquemare](#jean-vicquemare)

### Kim Kitsuragi

*Also: Kim, Kitsuragi, Lieutenant Kitsuragi*

**Your partner on the case: a calm, methodical RCM lieutenant from Precinct 57.**

Kim was assigned to work the hanging with you. He is precise, patient, keeps meticulous notes and treats you with dry professionalism even at your worst. He drives a Kineema motor carriage, which he loves. He is of Seolite descent, which some people in Martinaise are openly hostile about.

See also: [Precinct 57](#precinct-57), [Kineema](#kineema), [Seol](#seol), [Revachol Citizens Militia](#revachol-citizens-militia)

### Klaasje

*Also: Miss Oranje Disco Dancer*

**A charismatic woman staying on the Whirling's top floor, often smoking on her balcony.**

Klaasje is witty, worldly and hard to read. Her balcony overlooks the yard where the body was found.

See also: [Whirling-in-Rags](#whirling-in-rags), [Oranje](#oranje), [The Hanged Man](#the-hanged-man)

### Lena

*Also: Lena the Cryptozoologist's Wife, Cryptozoologist's Wife*

**A kind, elderly woman at the Whirling-in-Rags, married to a cryptozoologist.**

Lena loves to talk about cryptids, especially the legendary Insulindian Phasmid her husband is searching for. She is one of the gentlest people in Martinaise.

See also: [Insulindian Phasmid](#insulindian-phasmid), [Cryptozoology](#cryptozoology), [Whirling-in-Rags](#whirling-in-rags)

### Measurehead

**A towering bouncer who lectures anyone who will listen on 'race theory'.**

Measurehead is a hulking man who explains the world through pseudo-scientific racial theories. The game presents his ideas as absurd and wrong, and lets you argue with him.

*Real-world inspiration:* His name points at phrenology and craniometry: discredited 19th-century pseudosciences that 'measured heads' to justify racism.

See also: [Fascism](#fascism)

### René Arnoux

*Also: René, Rene Arnoux*

**An old veteran who plays pétanque. He fought for the royalists during the Revolution.**

René is proud, grumpy and still loyal to the old monarchy that the communist revolution overthrew decades ago. He plays pétanque with his friend Gaston.

See also: [Gaston Martin](#gaston-martin), [Royalists](#royalists), [Revolution](#revolution), [Pétanque](#pétanque)

### The Hanged Man

*Also: hanged man, the corpse*

**The murder victim: a man found hanging from a tree in the yard behind the Whirling-in-Rags.**

Solving who he was and how he died is the main case of the game. He has been hanging there for days; the investigation should have started long before you woke up.

See also: [Hardie Boys](#hardie-boys), [Whirling-in-Rags](#whirling-in-rags), [Mercenaries](#mercenaries)

<details><summary>Spoiler</summary>

He was a mercenary commander who arrived with the company's security force. He was already dead when he was hanged: someone shot him first.

</details>

### Titus Hardie

*Also: Titus*

**The leader of the Hardie Boys, a group of dockworkers who hang around the Whirling-in-Rags.**

Titus is a big, gruff union man who speaks for the Hardie Boys and is protective of his crew.

See also: [Hardie Boys](#hardie-boys), [Union](#union)

## Places

### Dolorian Church of Humanity

*Also: the Church, Dolorian church*

**An old, abandoned church in Martinaise.**

The church was built for the Dolorian faith, named after the Innocence Dolores Dei. It has long since stopped being used for worship.

See also: [Dolores Dei](#dolores-dei), [Dolorian](#dolorian), [Innocence](#innocence)

### Doomed Commercial Area

**A row of shopfronts where every business seems to fail. Locals believe the place is cursed.**

### Fishing Village

**A small coastal community at the edge of Martinaise.**

See also: [Martinaise](#martinaise)

### Harbour

*Also: Harbor, the harbour, the harbor*

**The Martinaise docks, controlled by the dockworkers' union and currently shut down by a strike.**

See also: [Union](#union), [Evrart Claire](#evrart-claire), [Strike](#strike), [Wild Pines](#wild-pines)

### Jamrock

**A rough district of Revachol, home of Harry's Precinct 41.**

See also: [Precinct 41](#precinct-41), [Revachol](#revachol)

### Martinaise

**The poor harbour district of Revachol where the whole game takes place.**

Martinaise is a run-down neighbourhood of docks, shuttered shops, a fishing village and an abandoned church. The dockworkers' union is the real power here, and the district is in the middle of a strike.

See also: [Revachol](#revachol), [Harbour](#harbour), [Union](#union), [Whirling-in-Rags](#whirling-in-rags)

*Also: 41st Precinct, 41st, Forty-first*

See also: [Revachol Citizens Militia](#revachol-citizens-militia), [Jamrock](#jamrock), [Jean Vicquemare](#jean-vicquemare), [Judit Minot](#judit-minot)

### Precinct 57

*Also: 57th Precinct, 57th*

**Kim Kitsuragi's police station.**

See also: [Revachol Citizens Militia](#revachol-citizens-militia), [Kim Kitsuragi](#kim-kitsuragi)

### Revachol

*Also: Revacholian, Revacholians, Revachol West*

**The city where the game takes place: a war-scarred former capital, now run by foreign powers.**

Revachol was once the grand capital of a kingdom. About fifty years ago a communist revolution took over the city and declared a Commune; an alliance of foreign nations, the Coalition, crushed it. Ever since, Revachol has been administered under the Coalition's rule: poor, partly ruined, and still full of people who remember the Revolution.

The game takes place in one small district of it, Martinaise.

*Real-world inspiration:* Revachol mixes Paris (its French-flavoured names and the Paris Commune of 1871) with the decline of post-Soviet port cities in Eastern Europe.

See also: [Martinaise](#martinaise), [Coalition](#coalition), [Revolution](#revolution), [Commune](#commune), [Insulinde](#insulinde)

### Whirling-in-Rags

*Also: the Whirling, Whirling*

**The cheap hostel and cafeteria where you wake up. The body hangs in the yard behind it.**

A combined bar, cafeteria and hostel managed by Garte. You've been staying (and wrecking things) upstairs, and you need to pay for your room. Many important characters are inside or nearby.

See also: [Garte](#garte), [The Hanged Man](#the-hanged-man), [Martinaise](#martinaise)

## World

### Aerostatic

*Also: aerostatics, aerostat, aerostats*

**An airship. Aerostatics are how people and goods fly across the world, even over the Pale.**

See also: [Pale](#pale)

### Dolores Dei

**The most revered Innocence in history, a near-saintly woman of centuries past.**

Dolores Dei is remembered as a figure of grace and compassion. A whole faith, the Dolorian church, grew around her.

*Real-world inspiration:* Her iconography echoes the Virgin Mary and Joan of Arc. 'Dolores' is Spanish for 'sorrows'; 'Dei' is Latin for 'of God'.

See also: [Innocence](#innocence), [Dolorian](#dolorian), [Dolorian Church of Humanity](#dolorian-church-of-humanity)

### Dolorian

*Also: Dolorianism*

## Factions

### Coalition

*Also: Coalition of Nations, the Coalition*

**The alliance of wealthy nations that crushed Revachol's revolution and now governs the city.**

About fifty years ago, the Coalition invaded Revachol and destroyed the communist Commune. Since then the city has been administered under Coalition rule, guided by the Moralintern. The city has stayed poor and half-ruined ever since.

*Real-world inspiration:* Foreign powers that intervene in other countries' revolutions, and international institutions that manage struggling economies.

### Moralintern

*Also: Moral International*

**The 'Moral International': the supranational body behind the Coalition, preaching moderate centrism.**

The Moralintern presents itself as the reasonable, peaceful middle ground of world politics. In practice it protects the status quo.

*Real-world inspiration:* The name parodies the 'Comintern' (Communist International); what it does resembles the UN, the IMF and the EU rolled into one.

### Revachol Citizens Militia

*Also: RCM, Citizens Militia, the Militia*

The RCM began as a volunteer citizens' militia and became the city's de facto police. Its authority is limited: the Coalition holds real power, and in Martinaise the union often does too.

*Real-world inspiration:* In the Soviet Union and other Eastern Bloc states, the police were officially called the 'militia' (militsiya).

## History

### Commune

*Also: the Commune, Revacholian Commune, Communard, Communards, communard, communards*

**The short-lived communist government of Revachol after the Revolution. Its members were Communards.**

The Commune ruled Revachol until the Coalition invaded and destroyed it. Its defeat shaped the city's present: foreign rule, poverty, and an RCM police force improvised from citizens.

*Real-world inspiration:* The Paris Commune of 1871, whose members were also called Communards and which was also crushed by force.

See also: [Revolution](#revolution), [Coalition](#coalition), [Communism](#communism)

### Revolution

*Also: the Revolution, Revacholian Revolution, the revolution*

**The communist uprising in Revachol, about fifty years before the game. It overthrew the monarchy.**

Communist revolutionaries took the city from its royal government and founded the Commune. Their victory was short-lived: the Coalition of foreign nations invaded and crushed it. The Revolution is still a living memory for older characters, and the scars (bullet holes, ruins, grudges) are everywhere.

*Real-world inspiration:* The Paris Commune of 1871 and the revolutions of the 20th century.

See also: [Commune](#commune), [Coalition](#coalition), [Communism](#communism), [Royalists](#royalists)

### Royalists

*Also: royalist, monarchist, monarchists*

**Supporters of Revachol's old monarchy, which the communist revolution overthrew.**

Some old veterans, like René Arnoux, fought for the king against the revolutionaries and still hold onto that loyalty.

See also: [Revolution](#revolution), [Reactionary](#reactionary)
